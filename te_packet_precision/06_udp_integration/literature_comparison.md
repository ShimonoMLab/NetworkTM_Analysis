# Targeted prior-art review: packet-aware directed dependence estimation
Review date: 2026-11-24. This is a targeted comparison using accessible article/abstract records, not a systematic review or proof of novelty. Searches covered missing-observation HAC and categorical series, transfer entropy and gaps, task-oriented communication, neural signal loss/recovery, communications topology inference and standards. The papers below set a conservative boundary on claims.

## Defensible contribution and assumptions
For one-lag binary TE, define C=(X_previous,Y_previous,Y_current), W=1 only when all needed samples arrive. The empirical TE is a smooth functional of the distribution of C at interior probabilities. Under independent stationary/mixing masks and signals, the centered influence score psi yields a masked long-run variance approximately [f gamma(0) + 2 sum q(k) gamma(k)]/(T f^2). This follows standard ratio linearization and amplitude-modulation arguments; it is not a general theorem for nonstationarity, zero cell probabilities, informative missingness or null-edge testing. At TE=0 the first derivative may degenerate, requiring separate calibration or second-order treatment. For observed-state informative missingness, correctly specified positive joint inclusion probabilities allow inverse probability weighting of category counts, an established statistical approach; nonlinear TE is not finite-sample unbiased. State metadata must remain available even when payload is missing and its cost needs accounting.

The defensible prospective contribution is *jointly evaluating packet organization, repair policy and inference uncertainty for directed dependence estimation under deadline and traffic constraints*, with explicit valid-sample correlations, bias, and detection metrics; it is a research hypothesis and integration, not a priority claim. A quantitative novelty assertion would require stronger bibliographic coverage and head-to-head baselines. For the application, cite the close categorical/HAC prior art, existing neural packet-loss work and task-oriented communication, and avoid 'first' or 'novel HAC' claims.
